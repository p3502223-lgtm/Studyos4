A uniform circular disc of mass M and outer radius R has a concentric hole of radius r. What is its moment of inertia about the perpendicular central symmetry axis?

auto_awesome Verified Solution Density σ = M / [π(R² - r²)]
I = ½ M (R² + r²)
  • check Inertia is calculated by subtracting smaller imaginary disk inertia: I = I_total - I_removed.
  • check Mass expands as ½ σ π (R⁴ - r⁴) = ½ σ π (R² - r²)(R² + r²).
  • lightbulb Factorisation results in an intuitive positive addition because M = σ π (R² - r²).
